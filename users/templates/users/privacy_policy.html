TutorGali ("we", "us", "our") operates the TutorGali website and connects students with tutors for tutoring services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or use TutorGali, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and mobile number
  • Address and location details (used for tutor search and matching)
  • Tutor profile details — qualifications, subjects, grades, rates, availability, and uploaded documents (certificates, ID proof) for verification
  • Booking requests and communication between students and tutors
  • Payment-related information for subscription payments (processed securely via Razorpay — we do not store your card, UPI, or bank details)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To match students with relevant tutors based on location, subject, and grade
  • To verify tutor identity and qualifications before approval
  • To process subscription payments and send related confirmations
  • To send OTPs, booking updates, and account-related notifications via email or SMS

3. Payment Information

All payments on TutorGali are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank account details on our servers — these are handled entirely by Razorpay.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your profile details (such as name, subjects, and location) may be shown to other users as necessary for the tutoring service to function (e.g. a tutor's public profile shown to students). We may share data with service providers (such as Razorpay for payments) only as needed to operate the platform.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Choices

You may update your profile information at any time by logging into your account. To request deletion of your account or data, contact us using the details on our Contact Us page.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of TutorGali after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
